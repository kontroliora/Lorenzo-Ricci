# Product photography

## Photo policy

Only photos of the actual physical item, taken for this site, are used in a
product's public gallery (`images` / `coverImage` in [`lib/products.ts`](../../lib/products.ts)).

Supplier or competitor photos — reference shots used during sourcing/cataloguing
that don't show the specific item being sold — are never published in a gallery,
even temporarily. Keep them out of `public/` entirely; if a working copy is
needed during processing, keep it in an untracked source folder and delete it
once the real product photos are in place.
